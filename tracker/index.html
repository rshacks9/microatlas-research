Methodology & honest caveats

  • Only official data. Police data comes from the City of Dallas OpenData portal; fire/EMS from Dallas Fire-Rescue's own active-incidents feed. This page never uses scanner transcriptions or crowdsourced apps, because those are unverified.
  • Live police calls are refreshed here every 2 minutes (matching the city's publish cadence). By city policy the feed only lists calls where an officer has arrived and that are legally releasable — some in-progress and sensitive calls are excluded by the city, not by this page.
  • Incident reports are the verified, written-up records (offense, NIBRS classification, block address). The city updates them daily, so the most recent 24–48 h will look artificially light. That lag is the price of "verified".
  • Locations are block-level as published by the city — precise addresses and victim details are withheld at the source.
  • All times are as published by the city (Central Time). Each feed's dot above shows green (fresh), amber (stale), or red (unreachable) with its last successful fetch.
  • This page has no backend and stores nothing: your browser talks straight to the city's servers.
